01 / About

Always raising the bar in research.

I've always been drawn to the stories behind the numbers. With over seven years of research experience across hospitality, retail, commercial real estate and financial markets, I combine quantitative rigor, qualitative insights and creative thinking to challenge conventional perspectives and uncover meaningful opportunities.

Most recently, I served as Manager of Capital Markets Research at JLL, leading regional and global research initiatives, publishing industry insights and advising clients and executives. Earlier in my career, I worked in real estate valuation, investment services and market research at CME Group.

I believe the best research goes beyond answering questions by challenging us to ask better ones, explore new analytical approaches and continually elevate the quality of our insights.
